Approach做法

Item response theory treats each model as an ability and each benchmark item as a difficulty, then updates both online as results arrive.

项目反应理论把每个模型看成一个能力值、每道题看成一个难度值，结果进来时在线更新两者。

Discipline纪律

Local holdout scores decide model choice; the leaderboard does not.

模型选择只看本地留出分数，不看排行榜。
