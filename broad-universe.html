Broad-universe A-share factor study宽基 A 股因子研究

The signal turned out to be real. The cost of hedging it turned out to be bigger.信号是真的，但对冲它的成本更大。

Universe标的
CSI broad indices沪深宽基
Honest trials诚实试验数
N = 67
Alpha t-statAlpha t 值
≈ 4.2
Verdict结论
Not tradable as built按现方案不可交易

What held up站得住的

Holding 300 names, the signal's alpha has a t-statistic around 4 with beta near 1, decile monotonicity of +0.99, and the same sign at 5-, 10-, 20- and 45-day rebalancing.

持仓 300 只时 alpha 的 t 值约 4，beta 接近 1，十分组单调性 +0.99，5、10、20、45 日调仓方向一致。

What did not站不住的

About three quarters of the spread lives on the short side, which A-shares make hard to trade. Hedging with index futures needs an information ratio of about 1.66; the measured one is about 1.1. After counting all 67 trials, the Deflated Sharpe is no longer significant.

约四分之三的区分能力在 A 股很难做的空头端。用股指期货对冲需要信息比率约 1.66，实测只有约 1.1。把 67 次试验全部计入后，Deflated Sharpe 不再显著。

Why it matters意义

The conclusion is a negative one, stated with the numbers that force it. Holdout data stays sealed until a forward record exists.

结论是否定的，并给出了逼出这个结论的数字。留存期数据在前向记录攒够之前一直封存。

Research and simulation only. No real capital. Factor construction is kept private.仅研究与模拟，无真实资金；因子构造不公开。
