Then, Again

A browser darkroom that puts your photo through real film stocks and past years, built into an interactive site about how companies sell the past again.一个浏览器里的暗房，能把你的照片放进真实胶片和过去的年代。它嵌在一个讲公司怎样把过去重新卖一遍的交互网站里。

The question问题

People in the past kept drawing the future. People now keep buying back the past. The site asks what a company keeps and what it changes when it sells an old object again.

过去的人一直在画未来，现在的人一直在把过去买回来。这个网站想看清一件事，公司把一件旧东西重新拿出来卖的时候，留下了什么，换掉了什么。

What you can do能做什么

Open a photo in the darkroom and pick a year. The picture goes through a film step and then a print step, with adjustable grain, halation and fade. You can add a 35mm film edge with perforations, burn in a date, compare before and after, and export the result. Each of the eight object pages hands its own year to the darkroom, so the essay and the tool lead into each other.

在暗房里打开一张照片，选一个年代。画面先过胶片再过相纸，颗粒和光晕都能调。可以加带齿孔的 35mm 片边，烧上日期，对比前后效果，再导出。八个物件页各自把自己的年代交给暗房，文章和工具互相带路。

What it found发现

In our eight cases the look was kept 8 times out of 8, and the working part was changed 6 times out of 7. The Polaroid camera is the exception, because its old working part is what people pay for.

在我们选的八个案例里，外观 8 次里保留了 8 次，真正干活的那部分 7 次里换掉了 6 次。宝丽来相机是例外，因为它卖的就是那个旧的工作方式。

How it was made制作

Katherine Liao and I chose the cases and wrote the argument from three course readings. I directed the design and the interaction, and built the site with Claude as a coding assistant. It uses no framework. The film stock looks use colour tables that I computed from the published spektrafilm film and paper profiles (CC BY-SA 4.0), with my own simplified version of the calculation. The darkroom code is offered under GPL-3.0-or-later, as that project asks. The photos come from Wikimedia Commons, and each one is credited with its licence.

案例和论点是我和 Katherine Liao 一起定的，依据是三篇课程阅读。设计和交互由我主导，代码是和 Claude 协作完成的，没有用任何框架。胶片效果用的色彩表是我根据 spektrafilm 公开的胶片与相纸数据（CC BY-SA 4.0）算出来的，计算部分是自己写的简化版。暗房代码按该项目的要求以 GPL-3.0-or-later 开放。照片来自 Wikimedia Commons，每张都标了作者和授权。

The 8 of 8 and 6 of 7 counts describe the eight cases we picked. They are not a sample of the market. The named film stocks come from published data run through a simplified calculation, so they are close to the real engine but not identical. The looks without a film name are tuned by eye. Six of the sixteen images are drawings made for the site.8/8 和 6/7 只是我们挑的这八个案例里的计数，不代表整个市场。带胶片名字的效果来自公开数据加简化计算，接近原引擎但不完全一致。没有胶片名字的年代效果是凭观感调的。十六张图里有六张是为网站画的示意图。
