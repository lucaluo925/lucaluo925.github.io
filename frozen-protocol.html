How much alpha survives a frozen protocol?冻结协议下还剩多少 Alpha？

Factor-mining agents make it cheap to try one more spec after seeing results. I measured what that costs.因子挖掘智能体让"看完结果再换个写法"几乎零成本。我量化了这件事的代价。

Question问题

When an agent can generate thousands of readable factor formulas, the reported winner hides a much larger family of discarded tries. Which rules have to be frozen, and when, for a reported result to mean what it says?

当智能体能生成成千上万个可读的因子公式时，最后报出来的那一个背后藏着一大堆被丢掉的尝试。要冻结哪些规则、在什么时候冻结，报出来的结果才算数？

Experiment实验

On a pure-null panel where every discovery is false by construction, 61 grammar-generated factors are tested under four rules across 200 replications. A preregistered Holm protocol over the full family reports a false discovery 1.0% of the time; testing each factor at 5% does so 65.0% of the time. Reporting the best of 128 hidden specifications per factor pushes it to 42.0%, and cutting the family to the two smallest p-values pushes it to 44.5%.

在一个按构造"所有发现都是假的"的纯零假设面板上，用 200 次重复，在四种规则下检验 61 个语法生成的因子。对整个家族做预注册 Holm 校正时，误报率 1.0%；每个因子单独按 5% 检验，误报率 65.0%。每个因子从 128 种隐藏写法里挑最好的，误报率升到 42.0%；看完结果把家族砍到 p 值最小的两个，升到 44.5%。

Why it happens原因

Dependence among formulaic factors shrinks 61 nominal tests to about 20.6 effective ones (Li–Ji estimator), which explains the naive rate. That number explains the result; it is not a licence to shrink the frozen family afterwards.

公式因子之间高度相关，61 个名义检验只相当于约 20.6 个独立检验（Li–Ji 估计），这解释了朴素检验的误报率。但这个数字只用来解释，不能拿来事后缩小已冻结的检验家族。

The protocol协议

A hash-bound frozen protocol for an A-share factor study: two candidates plus 59 historical tests fixed at m = 61, every candidate bound by SHA-256, a 2 × 86 × 60 response cube built once, and independent replay before anything is reported.

给一个 A 股因子研究设计的哈希绑定冻结协议：2 个新候选加 59 个历史检验，m 固定为 61；每个候选用 SHA-256 绑定，响应立方体 2 × 86 × 60 只构建一次，报告前必须独立复现。

Offline evidence only. No live trading or returns are claimed, and the real study was not rerun for the paper.只用离线证据，不声称实盘或收益；论文没有重跑真实研究。
