Design设计

Every generated line must cite an authorised fact; numbers are matched exactly against evidence; a deterministic policy layer reads no config and does no I/O.

每条生成内容都要引用已授权的事实，数字与证据逐字比对；确定性策略层不读配置、不做 I/O。

Offline and mock-ATS tests do not substitute for real logins, CAPTCHAs or submissions.离线和模拟 ATS 测试不能代替真实登录、验证码和提交。
