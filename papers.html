How Much Alpha Survives a Frozen Protocol? Quantifying Goalpost Moving in Agentic Factor Discovery

Agenthon 2026 · NeurIPS 2026 · Atlanta

Factor-mining agents make it cheap to try one more specification after seeing the results. On a pure-null panel, 61 grammar-generated factors are tested under four rules: a preregistered Holm protocol reports a false discovery in 1.0% of 200 replications, testing each factor at 5% in 65.0%, reporting the best of 128 hidden specifications in 42.0%, and cutting the family to the two smallest p-values in 44.5%. Dependence shrinks the family to 20.59 effective tests. The paper then describes a hash-bound frozen protocol for an A-share study and states what it can and cannot verify.

因子挖掘智能体让人看完结果后再试一种写法几乎没有成本。在纯零假设面板上用四种规则检验 61 个语法生成的因子：预注册 Holm 协议在 200 次重复中误报 1.0%，每个因子单独按 5% 检验误报 65.0%，从 128 种隐藏写法中挑最优误报 42.0%，把家族砍到 p 值最小的两个误报 44.5%。因子间的相关性把 61 个检验压缩成约 20.59 个有效检验。论文随后给出一个 A 股研究的哈希绑定冻结协议，并说明它能验证什么、不能验证什么。

Reading the Rule Instead of the Filing: A Linkbase-Native System for Financial Audit Verification, and Five Falsifications

FinReason Cup 2026 · IEEE Big Data Cup

A deterministic system for SEC XBRL audit verification that uses no model at inference time and reaches 90.74% development accuracy against a 33.09% rule baseline. The gain comes from resolving calculation locators through xlink:href and from recognising that three data-quality rules need three different computations. Five falsified hypotheses are reported in full.

一个不调用模型的 SEC XBRL 审计核验确定性系统，开发阶段准确率 90.74%，规则基线 33.09%。提升来自两点：通过 xlink:href 解析计算定位符，以及识别出三条数据质量规则需要三种不同的计算。五条被证伪的假设全部如实报告。

Grading Intelligence Without Mandating Ownership: China’s 2026 AI-Audio Standards as Architectural Industrial Policy

Yale Journal of International Affairs

China’s 2026 AI-terminal standards grade speakers and earphones from L1 to L3. A double-coded content analysis of 69 incremental requirements (98.6% coder agreement) shows semantic-agentic requirements rising from 11.8% of new L1 requirements to 56.0% at L3, while embodied-audio requirements fall from 41.2% to 24.0%. The testing rules accept licensed components and host devices, so the regime is ownership-neutral by design; yet every audio product in the first published results came from an organisation that drafted the standard or an affiliate of one.

中国 2026 年的 AI 终端标准把音箱和耳机从 L1 分到 L3。对 69 条增量要求做双人编码内容分析（编码一致率 98.6%）发现：语义与智能体类要求从 L1 新增要求的 11.8% 升到 L3 的 56.0%，具身音频类要求从 41.2% 降到 24.0%。测试规则接受授权组件和宿主设备，因此制度设计上不要求自有；但首批公布结果里的每一款音频产品，都来自参与起草标准的机构或其关联公司。
